Model files used by the optional demo detectors:

- fire_smoke.pt: mfranzon/fire-smoke-yolov8, classes fire and smoke, AGPL-3.0.
  https://huggingface.co/mfranzon/fire-smoke-yolov8
- weapons.pt: cosgun99/gun-knife-yolo11n, classes gun and knife, MIT.
  https://huggingface.co/cosgun99/gun-knife-yolo11n

These community models are for educational demonstrations. They can miss objects
or produce false positives and must not be relied on for fire alarms or security.
